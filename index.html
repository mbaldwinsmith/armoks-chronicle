    THE CHRONICLER’S FIELD GUIDE

    Bring the history.
    Keep the wonder.

    Armok’s Chronicle is being built as a browser-based companion to Dwarf Fortress Legends exports.

    Your export kit

    legends.xml CORE
    The historical figures, places, artifacts, and events that form the archive.
    legends_plus.xml
    DFHack’s supplementary records, relationships, and geographical information.
    world_map.csv
    Terrain tiles and elevation for the flat atlas and optional Raised Relief view.
    world_history.txt · world_sites_and_pops.txt
    Readable reports and population snapshots that enrich the world’s context.

    What works in this edition?

    Import standard and Plus XML, reopen stored worlds, browse record categories, and inspect their source fields. Search names and IDs, open record pages, follow explicit references, and browse related events. Figure pages include recorded life dates and relationships. Event summaries link participants, and timelines support type/year filters and a major-events preset. Explore terrain and sites in the atlas, including optional Raised Relief. The Book of Records offers eight evidence-linked rankings with explicit counting rules and tied ranks. Complete biographies and additional report features remain in development. Text reports are stored but not yet interpreted.

    See the full development checklist ↗

    Local by design

    Your selected exports stay in your browser. The preview reads only the beginning of each file. Choosing Import world reads the complete XML and stores records on this device. You can cancel imports and delete saved worlds.

    Armok’s Chronicle is an unofficial fan project, independent of Bay 12 Games and DFHack.

      Map controls and legend
      Drag to pan; use the wheel or +/− to zoom. Focus the map and use arrow keys to pan. Click a tile to inspect it. North is up on the flat atlas; X increases east, Y south.
      
        
      Biomes use distinct colours with names in the biome legend. Elevation palette: low blue → green → tan → high pale. Lakes use blue. Evilness and savagery: low green → high red. Settlement marker colours follow the selected ownership or civilisation legend. Elevation is shown in export units.
      
        
      
        
      
        
      Ownership and civilisation are separate export-time fields. Dots show settlements; they do not mark territorial borders. Circle: organisation reference; diamond: −1 (no recorded affiliation); square: missing affiliation. Colours are stable, with names in the legend.
      
        
      Faction legend and visibility
      
        
      Battles and conflict events
      Battles, attacks, destruction, plundering, wounds, deaths and abductions. Dates filter events; terrain and settlement ownership remain export-time snapshots. Event organisation references are independent of settlement faction visibility. Undated events are excluded by year bounds. Unknown locations remain in the linked list. Rivers use exported cardinal connections; road links are inferred between adjacent road tiles.
