    THE CHRONICLER’S FIELD GUIDE

    Bring the history.
    Keep the wonder.

    Armok’s Chronicle is being built as a browser-based companion to Dwarf Fortress Legends exports.

    Your export kit

    legends.xml CORE
    The historical figures, places, artifacts, and events that form the archive.
    legends_plus.xml
    DFHack’s supplementary records, relationships, and geographical information.
    world_map.csv
    Terrain tiles and elevation for the flat atlas and optional Raised Relief view.
    world_history.txt · world_sites_and_pops.txt
    Readable reports and population snapshots that enrich the world’s context.

    What works in this edition?

    Navigate the archive shell, choose a reading theme, and preview local file headers and sizes. Parsing, persistent worlds, search, maps, and stories are scheduled in the development plan.

    See the full development checklist ↗

    Local by design

    Your selected exports stay in your browser. The preview reads only the beginning of each file. A future import will read the full export and store its records on this device.

    Armok’s Chronicle is an unofficial fan project, independent of Bay 12 Games and DFHack.
